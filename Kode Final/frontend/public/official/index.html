Capabilities

Everything You Need, One Voice Command Away

Deep integration with Microsoft 365 to handle your daily workplace tasks.

Intelligent Email Management

Read, compose, send, search, and delete emails without opening Outlook. Ashistanto resolves contacts by name and formats professional HTML emails automatically.

  • Send emails by recipient name
  • Auto-formatted with professional styling
  • Search and summarize key emails
  • Delete sent emails with confirmation

“Send an email to Sarah about the project deadline”

Email sent to Sarah — Subject: “Project Deadline Reminder”

Effortless Calendar & Meetings

Schedule, view, update, and cancel calendar events. Ashistanto auto-creates Teams meeting links and resolves attendees by name.

  • Create Teams meetings with auto-generated links
  • Add multiple attendees by name
  • View today’s and upcoming schedule
  • Reschedule or cancel meetings instantly

“Schedule a sync with the Finance team tomorrow at 3 PM”

Teams meeting created — Tomorrow 3:00 PM

Teams Messaging, Simplified

Send and read Teams messages by voice. Ashistanto reads recent chat context before replying so responses stay relevant.

  • Send chat messages to anyone by name
  • Read and summarize recent conversations
  • Context-aware replies with chat history
  • Delete messages when needed

“Tell John on Teams that the design is approved”

Teams message sent to John

Smart File Search & Reading

Find documents across OneDrive and SharePoint instantly. Ashistanto can also read and summarize PDF, Word, and Excel files.

  • Search files by name or keywords
  • Returns paths and direct open links
  • Read PDF, DOCX, and XLSX content
  • Works across OneDrive and SharePoint
